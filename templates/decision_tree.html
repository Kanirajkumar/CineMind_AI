How does a Decision Tree work?

01

Root Node

The starting point of the tree. It evaluates a feature and begins splitting the data into smaller groups.

02

Decision Branches

Branches represent the possible outcomes of a decision, such as whether a movie's rating is above a threshold.

03

Leaf Nodes

Leaf nodes provide the final predicted class, such as likely successful or may need improvement.

MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION

How does the tree choose a split?

1. Entropy

Entropy measures the uncertainty or impurity in a group of training examples.

H(S) = − Σ p(i) log₂ p(i)

Lower entropy means the group is more uniform. Entropy is zero when all examples belong to one class.

2. Gini Impurity

Gini impurity measures the chance of incorrectly classifying a randomly selected example if its class were assigned according to the group's class proportions.

Gini(S) = 1 − Σ p(i)²

Lower Gini impurity indicates a more uniform group. A pure group has a Gini value of zero.

3. Information Gain

Information Gain measures how much a split reduces uncertainty in the data.

IG = H(parent) − Σ [weighted child entropy]

A split with higher Information Gain reduces entropy more effectively.

Important: Entropy and Gini are alternative splitting criteria. The criterion actually used by CineMind AI depends on the configuration in model_utils.py. These formulas explain the concepts; they do not mean the model necessarily uses all three.
